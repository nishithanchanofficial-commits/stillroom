The local check validates file size, supported format, browser decoding, and image dimensions. It does not inspect pixel watermarks or verify provenance. Canvas exports are newly encoded and may not preserve embedded metadata or credentials.

What this editor does

Stillroom helps with visible edits—cropping and copying a selected patch from elsewhere in the image. It does not detect or remove embedded SynthID, C2PA credentials, or other provenance signals.
